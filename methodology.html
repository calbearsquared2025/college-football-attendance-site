How we count the crowd

This site compares reported FBS home attendance across schools, seasons and conferences. Sources and accounting rules vary by period. The methods below explain the comparisons and their limitations.

02 / DEFINITIONS

What the numbers mean

Reported attendance
The attendance announced by a school or recorded by an attributed source. Reporting practices can vary across schools and seasons.
School average
Total reported attendance divided by credited home games. NCAA annual figures follow the NCAA's published home-game credits; reconstructed 2021–2024 figures include documented alternate-site and postseason home credits. Calculations retain full precision until display.
Conference and custom-group average
Total attendance across eligible schools divided by their combined credited home games. Each game contributes equally, regardless of school.
Attendance change
The difference between selected averages, expressed in spectators per game or as a percentage of the starting average. Comparisons require qualified data in both periods and a positive starting average.
2025 versus 2026 YTD
Compares the full 2025 season average with the available 2026 home-game average. Differences reflect the partial schedule and are labeled provisional.
Missing values and zero
Unavailable years are omitted from calculations. An actual zero is included when supported by evidence that no spectators attended.
Rounding
Percentage changes display one decimal place. A nonzero change rounding to zero retains its sign (+0.0% or −0.0%); an exact zero is labeled unchanged.
Key Stats
Historical highs, lows and averages use qualified seasons weighted by home games. Biggest and smallest increases and decreases use eligible schools or complete conference cohorts at the selected endpoints, ranked by the chosen map metric.

05 / MATERIAL EXCEPTIONS

Corrections and known differences

2001

California's original attendance

Cal's university record reports 200,655 attendees across six home games, replacing a conflicting secondary transcription.

2004

Incomplete national reconciliation

Our reconstructed school records account for 669 home games and 30,256,163 attendees, compared with the NCAA's 672 games and 30,337,237 attendees. The three-game, 81,074-attendee difference remains unresolved.

2006

Louisiana–Monroe's published average

The NCAA lists five home games and 111,564 attendees but prints an average of 18,594. The chart uses 22,313 per game, calculated from the reported total and game count.

2015

Relocated South Carolina game

The NCAA national attendance total includes 42,058 attendees from a South Carolina home-designated game played at LSU. The school-season averages follow the NCAA's published school figures.

2021–24

Reconstructed attendance

University records supplement missing CFBD game attendance and resolve conflicting figures. FIU's 2021 season is excluded because its home attendance could not be verified. Hawaiʻi's three spectator-free games in 2021 are recorded as zero.

2025

NCAA national-summary difference

The 136 school entries total 881 games and 36,800,758 attendees; the same report's national summary lists 882 games and 36,867,987 attendees. The difference of one game and 67,229 attendees remains unexplained. The charts use the published school entries.

06 / CURRENT SEASON & SOURCES

2026 updates and original references

CFBD identifies completed regular-season home games and their home/neutral designations. Official conference and university reports provide attendance. Each reported crowd is tied to a specific game and source, and the cumulative weekly series updates as games are completed.

The 2026 annual historical series will use the NCAA's final school attendance report after its publication and review, incorporating the NCAA's credited-home accounting.
